Before you apply

Eligibility & important information

Final eligibility and loan terms are determined by the lending institution.

Details generally reviewed

  • Applicant's age and identity details
  • Employment type and work profile
  • Monthly income and existing obligations
  • Credit history and repayment capacity
  • Loan amount and lender-specific policy

Keep in mind

  • Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee approval.
  • Interest rate, tenure, fees and loan amount vary by lender.
  • Only share personal details with the applicant's consent.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords or PINs with anyone.
  • ALLMARK assistance does not replace the lender's credit assessment.
Application preparation

Documents you may be asked for

Exact requirements depend on the lender and applicant profile. Do not upload documents through this enquiry form.

Salaried applicants

Identity and address proof, recent salary slips, bank statements and employment details may be requested.

Self-employed applicants

Identity and address proof, business proof, bank statements and income/tax documents may be requested.
